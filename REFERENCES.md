# References and implementation notes

These papers and documentation guided the architecture and training experiments. The table connects each method to its use in this project. The [report](reports/Zicheng_Zhou_Deep_Learning_Project_1_Report.pdf) presents the main results, and the [archive](archive/README.md) contains the individual configurations.

| Method | Source | Use in this project |
|---|---|---|
| Residual connections | He, K., Zhang, X., Ren, S. & Sun, J. *Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition*. CVPR (2016). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385) | Residual learning is the basis of each model. |
| Pre-activation blocks | He, K., Zhang, X., Ren, S. & Sun, J. *Identity Mappings in Deep Residual Networks*. ECCV (2016). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.05027) | Batch normalization and ReLU precede each convolution in the main WRN blocks. |
| Wide ResNet | Zagoruyko, S. & Komodakis, N. *Wide Residual Networks*. BMVC (2016). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.07146) | Depth, width, and block allocation were adjusted within the five-million-parameter budget. |
| Squeeze-and-excitation | Hu, J., Shen, L. & Sun, G. *Squeeze-and-Excitation Networks*. CVPR (2018). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.01507) | Channel attention is part of the robust WRN family. |
| Stochastic depth | Huang, G., Sun, Y., Liu, Z., Sedra, D. & Weinberger, K. *Deep Networks with Stochastic Depth*. ECCV (2016). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.09382) | Per-sample DropPath was included in the regularization screen. |
| RandAugment | Cubuk, E. D., Zoph, B., Shlens, J. & Le, Q. V. *RandAugment: Practical automated data augmentation with a reduced search space*. CVPR Workshops (2020). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.13719) | The main WRN uses N = 2 and M = 7; the robust recipe uses M = 5. |
| AutoAugment | Cubuk, E. D., Zoph, B., Mane, D., Vasudevan, V. & Le, Q. V. *AutoAugment: Learning Augmentation Policies from Data*. CVPR (2019). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.09501) | The CIFAR-10 policy was compared with RandAugment. |
| Mixup | Zhang, H., Cisse, M., Dauphin, Y. N. & Lopez-Paz, D. *mixup: Beyond Empirical Risk Minimization*. ICLR (2018). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.09412) | Training images and target distributions are mixed linearly. |
| CutMix | Yun, S., Han, D., Oh, S. J., Chun, S., Choe, J. & Yoo, Y. *CutMix: Regularization Strategy to Train Strong Classifiers with Localizable Features*. ICCV (2019). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.04899) | Image patches are exchanged and targets are weighted by the actual patch area. |
| Cutout | DeVries, T. & Taylor, G. W. *Improved Regularization of Convolutional Neural Networks with Cutout*. arXiv (2017). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.04552) | Masking was tested in the augmentation experiments. |
| Random Erasing | Zhong, Z., Zheng, L., Kang, G., Li, S. & Yang, Y. *Random Erasing Data Augmentation*. arXiv (2017). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.04896) | Randomly erased regions were tested as another augmentation choice. |
| Label smoothing | Szegedy, C., Vanhoucke, V., Ioffe, S., Shlens, J. & Wojna, Z. *Rethinking the Inception Architecture for Computer Vision*. CVPR (2016). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00567) | The main recipe uses 0.05 smoothing; a later matched continuation tested a value of zero. |
| Cosine learning rate | Loshchilov, I. & Hutter, F. *SGDR: Stochastic Gradient Descent with Warm Restarts*. ICLR (2017). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.03983) | Training uses warmup followed by a single cosine decay. |
| AdamW | Loshchilov, I. & Hutter, F. *Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization*. ICLR (2019). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05101) | Decoupled weight decay was tested in the optimizer screen. |
| SAM | Foret, P., Kleiner, A., Mobahi, H. & Neyshabur, B. *Sharpness-Aware Minimization for Efficiently Improving Generalization*. ICLR (2021). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.01412) | The optimizer screen included a two-pass sharpness-aware update. |
| Knowledge distillation | Hinton, G., Vinyals, O. & Dean, J. *Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network*. arXiv (2015). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531) | A previously trained project model supplied soft targets for the student. |
| EMA | [PyTorch weight-averaging documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/optim.html#weight-averaging-swa-and-ema) | Exponential moving averages of the weights were evaluated alongside the raw model. |
| SWA | Izmailov, P., Podoprikhin, D., Garipov, T., Vetrov, D. & Wilson, A. G. *Averaging Weights Leads to Wider Optima and Better Generalization*. UAI (2018). [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05407) | Late checkpoints were averaged along the cosine schedule, followed by batch-normalization recalibration. |
| AMP | [PyTorch AMP documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/amp.html) | BF16 mixed precision and channels-last layout were used for the main WRN training runs. |
| Lookahead | M. R. Zhang, J. Lucas, G. Hinton, and J. Ba. Lookahead Optimizer: k Steps Forward, 1 Step Back. NeurIPS, 2019. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.08610) | The Efficient ResNet experiments use Lookahead with SGD. |
| AugMix | D. Hendrycks et al. AugMix: A Simple Data Processing Method to Improve Robustness and Uncertainty. ICLR, 2020. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.02781) | AugMix image augmentation was tested in a separate training branch. |
| Quantization | B. Jacob et al. Quantization and Training of Neural Networks for Efficient Integer-Arithmetic-Only Inference. CVPR, 2018. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.05877) | An INT8 deployment experiment compared model size, CPU throughput, and validation accuracy. |
| Adam | D. P. Kingma and J. Ba. Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization. ICLR, 2015. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6980) | Adam was included in the optimizer screen. |
| Efficient ResNets | A. Thakur, H. Chauhan, and N. Gupta. Efficient ResNets: Residual Network Design. arXiv:2306.12100, 2023. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12100) | The uploaded architecture was trained with its author recipe and then with Mixup. |

The original Efficient ResNet code and its MIT license are credited in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Environment setup follows the [PyTorch installation instructions for the recorded version](https://pytorch.org/get-started/previous-versions/).
